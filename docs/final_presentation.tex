\begin{frame}{Problem Statement}
\sectiontag{The Challenge}

\textbf{\color{Primary}\normalsize Pharmaceutical Last-Mile Delivery}

\vspace{0.05cm}
\footnotesize
Pharmaceutical delivery operations involve multiple destinations, shipment
requirements and time constraints. Efficient planning becomes difficult when
travel conditions and operational resources vary.

\vspace{0.15cm}

\begin{columns}[T,totalwidth=\textwidth]
    \begin{column}{0.52\textwidth}
        \textbf{\color{Primary}\small Key operational challenges addressed}

        \begin{itemize}
            \setlength{\itemsep}{0pt}
            \item Multiple delivery locations and shipment demands
            \item Weight ($600\text{ kg}$) and volume ($3.0\text{ m}^3$) cargo limits
            \item Strict hospital time windows and unloading service times
            \item Driver shift limits ($6\text{ hours}$ baseline cutoff)
            \item Traffic uncertainty across urban roads
            \item Balancing route efficiency with delivery service quality
        \end{itemize}
    \end{column}

    \begin{column}{0.45\textwidth}
        \textbf{\color{Primary}\small Core problem}

        \vspace{0.04cm}
        \footnotesize
        How can pharmaceutical deliveries be planned efficiently while
        satisfying operational constraints?

        \vspace{0.2cm}
        \textbf{\color{Primary}\small What we built}

        \vspace{0.04cm}
        \footnotesize
        An interactive decision-support system using Operations
        Research to generate, evaluate, and stress-test feasible delivery plans in real time.
    \end{column}
\end{columns}
\end{frame}

\sectiontag{Why This Matters}

\begin{center}
\footnotesize
This challenge is critical because effective delivery planning requires simultaneously balancing \textbf{efficiency}, \textbf{service reliability}, \textbf{resource constraints}, and \textbf{traffic uncertainty}.

\begin{columns}[T,totalwidth=\textwidth]

% LEFT COLUMN

\begin{column}{0.47\textwidth}

\textbf{\normalsize\color{Primary}Delivery efficiency}

\vspace{0.04cm}

\footnotesize

and vehicle wear required to complete the same set of deliveries.

\vspace{0.25cm}

\textbf{\normalsize\color{Primary}Service reliability}

\vspace{0.04cm}

\footnotesize

\begin{column}{0.47\textwidth}

\textbf{\normalsize\color{Primary}Resource utilisation}

\vspace{0.04cm}

\vspace{0.25cm}

\textbf{\normalsize\color{Primary}Traffic uncertainty}

\vspace{0.04cm}

\begin{frame}{Research Findings: Routing \& Allocation (RQ1, RQ2, RQ3)}
\sectiontag{Answers to Operational Questions}

\vspace{-0.15cm}

\begin{columns}[T,totalwidth=\textwidth]

    \begin{column}{0.485\textwidth}
        \begin{beamercolorbox}[rounded=true,shadow=false,wd=\linewidth,sep=4.5pt]{block body}
            {\bfseries\color{Primary}\small 01. Routing (Answered)}\par\vspace{0.04cm}
            \footnotesize
            \begin{itemize}
                \setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}
                \scriptsize
                \item Clustering stops into compact geographic zones reduced uncoordinated fleet distance by \textbf{$14.2\%$}.
                \item Day 1 baseline: \textbf{$369.7\text{ km}$} across 78 deliveries with $100\%$ time-window compliance.
                \item Sequencing ensures tight turnaround with zero route discontinuity.
            \end{itemize}
        \end{beamercolorbox}

        \vspace{0.12cm}

        \begin{beamercolorbox}[rounded=true,shadow=false,wd=\linewidth,sep=4.5pt]{block body}
            {\bfseries\color{Primary}\small 02. Resource Allocation (Answered)}\par\vspace{0.04cm}
            \footnotesize
            \begin{itemize}
                \setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}
                \scriptsize
                \item Dual knapsack constraints strictly maintained: weight ($\le 600\text{ kg}$) and volume ($\le 3.0\text{ m}^3$).
                \item Achieved \textbf{$78.4\%$ average vehicle capacity utilization}, preventing empty or under-utilized runs.
            \end{itemize}
        \end{beamercolorbox}
    \end{column}

    \begin{column}{0.485\textwidth}
        \begin{beamercolorbox}[rounded=true,shadow=false,wd=\linewidth,sep=4.5pt]{block body}
            {\bfseries\color{Primary}\small 03. Operational Decisions (Answered)}\par\vspace{0.04cm}
            \footnotesize
            \begin{itemize}
                \setlength{\itemsep}{2pt}
                \scriptsize
                \item The solver dynamically determines the \textbf{minimum active fleet size} needed to complete all deliveries within shift limits.
                \item Baseline conditions activate \textbf{4 vehicles}.
                \item When shift limits are restricted or traffic surges, the model automatically dispatches additional vehicles to preserve feasibility.
            \end{itemize}
        \end{beamercolorbox}

% 11. MATHEMATICAL MODEL
% ------------------------------------------------------------

\begin{frame}{Mathematical Model Formulation}

\sectiontag{The Optimisation Model}

\vspace{0.05cm}

\small

\begin{columns}[T,totalwidth=\textwidth]


% LEFT COLUMN
\begin{column}{0.48\textwidth}

\textbf{\color{Primary}\small Decision Variables}

\vspace{0.05cm}

\[
\scriptsize
x_{ijk} \in \{0, 1\}
\]
\footnotesize
Binary routing variable: $1$ if vehicle $k$ travels directly from $i$ to $j$; $0$ otherwise.

\vspace{0.05cm}

\[
\scriptsize
y_{ik} \in \{0, 1\}
\]
\footnotesize
Assignment variable: $1$ if vehicle $k$ serves customer $i$; $0$ otherwise.

\vspace{0.05cm}

\[
\scriptsize
T_i \ge 0, \quad L_i \ge 0
\]
\footnotesize
Arrival time $T_i$ and deadline lateness penalty $L_i = \max(0, T_i - l_i)$.

\end{column}


% RIGHT COLUMN
\begin{column}{0.48\textwidth}

\textbf{\color{Primary}\small Objective Function}

\vspace{0.05cm}

\[
\scriptsize
\min Z = \alpha \left(\frac{D}{D_0}\right) + \beta \left(\frac{T}{T_0}\right) + \gamma \left(\frac{L}{L_0}\right)
\]

\vspace{-0.05cm}

\footnotesize
where:
\begin{itemize}
\setlength\itemsep{1pt}
\scriptsize
\item $D$ = Total fleet travel distance (km)
\item $T$ = Total transit time (min)
\item $L$ = Total lateness penalty (min)

\begin{frame}{Key Operational Discovery: Shift Limits vs.\ Traffic Shock}
\sectiontag{An Counter-Intuitive Operational Finding}

\vspace{-0.15cm}

\begin{center}
    \footnotesize
    \textbf{Question:} When traffic doubles ($2.0\times$ congestion surge), what happens if we reduce the driver shift limit from 8 hours to 4.5 hours?
\end{center}

\vspace{0.05cm}

\begin{table}[ht]
    \centering
    \scriptsize
    \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.12}
    \begin{tabular}{lccc}
        \toprule
        \textbf{Operational Metric} & \textbf{8.0-Hour Shift (480 min)} & \textbf{4.5-Hour Shift (270 min)} & \textbf{Operational Trade-Off} \\
        \midrule
        \textbf{Traffic Congestion Multiplier} & $2.00\times$ (Severe Shock) & $2.00\times$ (Severe Shock) & Identical traffic condition \\
        \textbf{Demanded Orders Served} & \textbf{78 / 78} ($100\%$) & \textbf{78 / 78} ($100\%$) & Complete coverage verified \\
        \textbf{Vans Dispatched} & \textbf{6 vans} & \textbf{12 vans} & Fleet parallelization \\
        \textbf{Late Deliveries} & \textbf{13 delayed stops} & \textbf{0 delayed stops} & \textbf{All delays eliminated!} \\
        \textbf{On-Time Delivery Rate (OTR)} & \textbf{83.3\%} & \textbf{100.0\%} & $\mathbf{+16.7\%}$ punctuality gain \\
        \textbf{Fleet Operating Cost} & \textbf{\euro 988} & \textbf{\euro 1,234} & $+24.9\%$ operational expenditure \\
        \textbf{Max Individual Route Duration} & $458\text{ min}$ ($\le 480\text{ min}$) & $\mathbf{270\text{ min}}$ ($\le 270\text{ min}$) & Strict shift adherence \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
\end{table}

\vspace{0.05cm}

\begin{beamercolorbox}[rounded=true,shadow=false,wd=\linewidth,sep=4pt]{block body}
    {\bfseries\color{Primary}\scriptsize Managerial Insight: Fleet Parallelization}\par\vspace{0.02cm}
    \scriptsize
    Under heavy traffic, long routes suffer from \textbf{cascading delay accumulation}---early traffic jams cause downstream deliveries to miss their deadlines. Tightening shift limits forces the optimizer to deploy \textbf{12 shorter, parallel routes}, eliminating delay cascades and ensuring \textbf{100\% on-time delivery} for critical medical supplies at a $+24.9\%$ cost premium.
\end{beamercolorbox}
