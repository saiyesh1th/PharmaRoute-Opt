Algorithmic Scalability & Combinatorial Intractability (RQ2/3)

Empirical evidence justifying the transition from Monolithic MILP to Cluster-First Decomposition.

Core Operations Research Insight:
While the monolithic MILP achieves high quality on small instances (n ≤ 20), at production scale (n ≥ 40), the formulation is computationally challenging under standard time limits, discovering 0 feasible integer tours within allocated budgets (60s–120s). This computational wall provides the empirical foundation for our feasibility-aware cluster-first decomposition.

📖 How PharmaRoute-Opt Works

1. The Clinical Problem

Pharmaceutical supply chains differ from typical e-commerce delivery: hospital orders have rigid delivery deadlines, vehicles carry cold-chain dual-knapsack weight (600 kg) and volume (3.0 m³) capacity limits, and traffic congestion can spoil temperature-sensitive medications.

2. The Operations Research Architecture

We formulate the problem as a CVRPTW-S (Capacitated Vehicle Routing with Time Windows and Service Times). Due to the combinatorial explosion of monolithic solvers at n ≥ 40 stops, we introduce Feasibility-Aware Cluster Decomposition, which partitions the distance matrix into capacity-compliant sectors and solves each using exact HiGHS branch-and-cut optimization.

3. Multi-Scenario Traffic Validation

Every route is evaluated across 3 real-world traffic regimes from the Athens delivery dataset (Optimistic, Most-Likely, Pessimistic) to stress-test real-world resilience.
